Settings – HandBrake TSD Helper

Jobs Settings

Encoding Settings

Control how many CPU threads HandBrake can use during encoding. Higher values = faster encoding, but heavier CPU load.

Tip:
• 0 or blank = let HandBrake decide automatically.
• A good starting point is your machine’s physical core count.
